NASDAQ 100 — Triangle Breakout Toward 37,000

Market: NASDAQ 100 / US100
Date: October 8, 2026
Focus: Thursday Market Focus

For this Thursday Market Focus, I am looking at the NASDAQ 100 / US100 and the current market structure around the key support zones and the recent triangle breakout.

The market has recently formed a triangle pattern. The market has now broken upward from this pattern, and from the current structure, the breakout could potentially open the way for a larger upward move toward the 37,000 area.

The important part of this analysis is not simply the breakout itself, but whether the market can maintain the structure after the breakout. The support zones below remain important areas to watch if price moves back toward them.

Current Market Structure

The key development on the chart is the triangle pattern and its upward breakout. The market has moved out of this structure to the upside, which creates the bullish scenario I am watching for today.

From a market-structure perspective, the breakout needs to be respected as a conditional development rather than treated as a guaranteed continuation. The market could potentially continue higher if the breakout structure holds.

26,000–27,000 — Immediate Support Zone

The first important support area in this analysis is the 26,000–27,000 range. This is the immediate support zone I am watching below the current breakout structure.

If the market moves back toward this area, its reaction around the support zone becomes important. A sustained structure above this zone would keep the upward scenario relevant, while a break through support would require the market structure to be reassessed.

22,000–23,000 — Major Support Zone

Beyond the immediate 26,000–27,000 support zone, the next major area I am watching is the 22,000–23,000 range.

This is the second major support area in the analysis. If the market were to move significantly lower and break through the first support zone, this 22,000–23,000 area becomes the next important level to monitor.

For the current bullish scenario, these support levels provide the structural areas to watch rather than levels to ignore if the market pulls back.

Potential Move Toward 37,000

The main scenario I am watching today is an upward continuation following the recent triangle breakout. If the breakout structure holds, the NASDAQ 100 could potentially continue upward and reach the 37,000 area.

This is the upside scenario from the current structure. It should remain conditional on the breakout holding and the market continuing to respect the structure that has developed.

Bullish Continuation Scenario
