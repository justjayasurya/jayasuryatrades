Monday Market Focus
Gold / XAUUSD — Head & Shoulders Breakdown

Market: Gold / XAUUSD
Date: October 5, 2026
Focus: Monday Market Focus

For this Monday Market Focus, I am looking at Gold / XAUUSD and the current market structure.

The market is forming a Head & Shoulders pattern and has already broken out of that pattern on the daily timeframe to the downside.

From the current market structure, there is a high possibility that Gold could move lower toward the $4,000–$4,100 price zone.

The $4,000–$4,100 zone is a huge support area I am watching.

Because of the Head & Shoulders downside breakout, the market could potentially move toward this support zone.
Chart 1 — Daily Head & Shoulders downside breakout and the $4,000–$4,100 support zone.

Head & Shoulders Breakdown

The important development I am watching is the Head & Shoulders pattern breakdown on the daily timeframe.

The breakout has occurred toward the downside. Because of this structure, I am not looking to force buying trades from the current market area.

The potential downside area I am watching is the major $4,000–$4,100 support zone.

Major Support Zone
$4,000–$4,100

This is a major area of interest because the market could potentially move toward this support after the Head & Shoulders breakdown.

Why I Am Not Looking for Buy Trades Now

At the current stage, I do not consider it the right time to look for buying trades.

The Head & Shoulders pattern has already broken downward on the daily timeframe, while the major support zone is still below the current market area.

Instead of trying to anticipate a reversal, I would rather wait for the market structure to provide confirmation.

No forced buying.

The downside structure needs to be respected while the market remains below the confirmation I am waiting for.
Chart 2 — Descending trendline structure and the upside breakout confirmation being watched.

The Descending Trendline

The market is also forming a descending trendline pattern.

Previously, the market formed a descending trendline and, after breaking that trendline upward, produced a huge rally and strong upward movement.

A similar descending trendline is forming again, and this is the key confirmation I am watching for a possible bullish move.

What Happens If the Trendline Breaks Up?

If the current descending trendline breaks upward, there could be a strong upward move. That would be the point where I would start looking for buy setups and buy entries.

The important point is that the trendline breakout needs to happen first. Until that confirmation appears, I do not want to anticipate the bullish move.

Descending trendline breaks upward

→ Bullish momentum could develop

→ That is the area where I would start looking for buy setups and entries.

The Two Scenarios I Am Watching

Bearish Scenario

The current Head & Shoulders structure has already broken downward on the daily timeframe. If the downside structure continues, Gold could potentially move toward the major $4,000–$4,100 support zone.

Bullish Scenario

If the descending trendline breaks upward, I would then start watching for bullish setups and possible buy entries. The previous trendline breakout produced a strong upward move, which is why this trendline is important to monitor.

Why Patience Matters Here

At the moment, I do not see it as the right time to buy while the Head & Shoulders structure remains unresolved and the descending trendline has not broken upward.

At the same time, I also do not consider it the right time to sell aggressively because the major $4,000–$4,100 support zone is immediately below the current market area.

This creates a situation where confirmation is more important than prediction.

Key Takeaways

  • The market is forming a Head & Shoulders pattern.
  • The Head & Shoulders pattern has broken downward on the daily timeframe.
  • The major support zone I am watching is $4,000–$4,100.
  • Because of the current downside structure, I am not looking for buying trades at this stage.
  • The market is also forming a descending trendline.
  • A previous descending trendline breakout to the upside produced a huge rally.
  • A similar trendline is forming again.
  • An upside break of the current descending trendline would be the confirmation I am watching for possible buy setups.
  • Until that upside trendline breakout happens, I prefer patience rather than forcing a buy.
  • I also do not want to sell aggressively while the major $4,000–$4,100 support is immediately below.

The objective is not to predict every movement. It is to wait for the market to show the direction, confirm the structure, and then act according to the setup.
